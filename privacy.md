# Privacy Policy - Proxmox Workstation Backups

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Proxmox Workstation Backups is a personal tool used only by its owner to back up the owner's own Proxmox workstation data to the owner's own Google Drive account. This policy explains how Google Drive access and backup data are handled.

## Google Drive access

The application requests access to Google Drive only so that it can upload encrypted backup files to, and read them back from, the owner's own Drive account for recovery verification. It does not access Drive content for any other purpose.

## Encrypted backup storage

Backups are encrypted on the owner's machine before upload. Only encrypted backup files are stored in Google Drive. The encryption key is held by the owner and is never uploaded or published.

## Owner-only use

The application is used only by its owner, on the owner's own account and data. It is not offered to the public and does not collect or process data belonging to any other person.

## No sale or public sharing

Backup data and Google user data are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties. They are never made public. They are not used for advertising, analytics, or training of AI or machine learning models.

## Data retention and deletion

Backups remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. The owner can delete backup files or revoke the application's access at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page.

## Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date.
